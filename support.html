常见问题

为什么调了手动快门或 ISO，照片变成了 12MP？
这是 iOS 的限制：48MP 只在全自动时可用。想要手动曝光又要高像素，请打开「高像素」模式。

为什么没有光圈调节？
iPhone 的镜头光圈是固定的。

「高像素」和「堆栈」有什么区别？
高像素是手持用的：一次连拍 8 张，利用手的微小抖动合成约 48MP 的照片。堆栈要把手机固定在三脚架上，连拍 8 张 48MP 合成，噪点更低。两者都需要几秒钟合成，完成前请不要退出 app。

有些功能我的手机上用不了？
这些功能要看机型：高像素需要能拍 RAW 的 iPhone；堆栈、旁轴模式和 ProRAW 格式需要支持 ProRAW 的 Pro 机型。

Stille 会收集我的数据吗？
不会。Stille 不联网，详见隐私政策。

FAQ

Why do my photos drop to 12 MP when I set shutter speed or ISO manually?
This is an iOS limitation: 48 MP is only available in full auto. For manual exposure at high resolution, turn on High-res mode.

Why is there no aperture control?
iPhone lenses have a fixed aperture.

What's the difference between High-res and Stack?
High-res is for handheld shooting: it takes a burst of 8 frames and uses natural hand motion to build a ~48 MP photo. Stack needs the phone on a tripod and merges 8 frames at 48 MP for lower noise. Both take a few seconds to process — please keep the app open until they finish.

Some features aren't available on my iPhone?
It depends on the model: High-res needs an iPhone that can capture RAW; Stack, Rangefinder mode and the ProRAW format need a Pro model with ProRAW support.

Does Stille collect my data?
No. Stille has no network access — see the Privacy Policy.
