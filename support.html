常见问题

自动和定影两个模式有什么区别？
自动模式是 iPhone 自己的成像，定影只提供拨盘和界面，照片和系统相机一样。定影模式从拍摄到成片都是我们自己的：按一下快门连拍一组 RAW，在手机上合成为一张底片，再由定影自己的显影引擎冲洗出来。想要画质、想手动曝光、想要 DNG 底片，用定影模式。

定影模式按下快门后为什么要等几秒？能退出吗？
合成一组连拍需要几秒，在后台进行，可以接着拍下一张。请等右下角的"合成中"消失再退出 app。

为什么调了手动快门或 ISO，照片变成了 12MP？
这是 iOS 的限制：在自动模式下，48MP 只在全自动时可用。定影模式不受这个限制，手动快门、ISO 下仍能得到 48MP 级的照片。

定影模式存什么格式？怎么留 DNG？
默认存 10 位 HEIF。在定影模式下点左上角的格式按钮切到 HEIF+RAW，会同时存一张合成后的 DNG 底片（48MP 约 42MB，27MP 约 25MB），可以在任何支持 DNG 的软件里后期。

「三脚架」开关是做什么的？
把手机固定在三脚架上时打开它，定影会改用全分辨率帧合成，照片更干净。手持也能用，但可能偏软。

为什么没有光圈调节？
iPhone 的镜头光圈是固定的。

有些功能我的手机上用不了？
定影模式的多帧合成需要能拍 RAW、内存足够的 iPhone。内存较小的机型只提供自动模式；能拍 RAW 但跑不动多帧的机型，定影模式会用单张 12MP 按同样方式显影。自动模式下的 ProRAW 格式需要支持 ProRAW 的 Pro 机型。

Stille 会收集我的数据吗？
不会。Stille 不联网，详见隐私政策。

FAQ

What's the difference between Auto and Develop?
Auto is the iPhone's own imaging; Stille only adds its dials and interface, and the photos match the system camera. Develop is ours from capture to finished photo: one press captures a burst of RAW frames, merges them on your iPhone into a single negative, and develops that negative with Stille's own engine. For image quality, manual exposure or a DNG negative, use Develop.

Why does Develop take a few seconds after I press the shutter? Can I leave the app?
Merging a burst takes a few seconds. It runs in the background, so you can keep shooting. Please wait until the "Merging" indicator in the corner disappears before leaving the app.

Why do my photos drop to 12 MP when I set shutter speed or ISO manually?
This is an iOS limitation: in Auto mode, 48 MP is only available in full auto. Develop mode isn't limited this way — you still get 48 MP-class photos with manual shutter speed and ISO.

What format does Develop save? How do I keep the DNG?
10-bit HEIF by default. In Develop mode, tap the format button at the top left and choose HEIF+RAW to also save the merged DNG negative (about 42 MB at 48 MP, 25 MB at 27 MP), which opens in any software that supports DNG.

What does the Tripod switch do?
Turn it on when the phone is on a tripod. Develop then merges full-resolution frames for a cleaner photo. It also works handheld, but results may be softer.

Why is there no aperture control?
iPhone lenses have a fixed aperture.

Some features aren't available on my iPhone?
The multi-frame merge in Develop mode needs an iPhone that can capture RAW and has enough memory. On iPhones with less memory only Auto is offered; on iPhones that can capture RAW but can't run the merge, Develop uses a single 12 MP frame developed the same way. The ProRAW format in Auto mode needs a Pro model with ProRAW support.

Does Stille collect my data?
No. Stille has no network access — see the Privacy Policy.
